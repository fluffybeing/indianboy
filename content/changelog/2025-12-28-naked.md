One of the most striking cultural differences Indianboy encounters in Sweden is the norm of walking naked in the apartment. In India, modesty is deeply ingrained in social behavior, and public nudity is taboo. However, in Sweden, the attitude towards nudity is much more relaxed, especially within the privacy of one's home. Indianboy finds it both liberating and perplexing to see Swedes casually strolling around their apartments without clothes.
